Our Heritage & Mission

Founded in 1912 by the Jamaica Baptist Union and named after the Calabar region of Nigeria, Calabar High School began with a clear mission: to provide sound secondary education grounded in Christian principles, discipline, and leadership.

Guided by our enduring motto, "The Utmost for the Highest," generations of Calabar men have distinguished themselves across academia, public service, business, and international athletics.



Our Core Values

  • Reverence for God: Putting God first in all things; academics, athletics, and personal life..

  • Discipline: Self-discipline and adherence to rules, order, and academic focus.

  • Professionalism: Conducting oneself with the standard expected of a young man preparing for adult and professional life.

  • Accountability: Owning one's actions, decisions, and their consequences.

  • Integrity: Moral honesty and consistency; doing what is right whether or not anyone is watching.

  • Respect: Respect for self, for others, and for authority.

  • School Pride: Our sense of ownership and loyalty to the institution, its history, traditions, and community.
